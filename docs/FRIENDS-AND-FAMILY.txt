Notebook Scanner - friends and family beta 0.5

For Android phones (Android 9 or newer) and Windows x64 laptops with Bluetooth.
Not an iPhone or Mac app. No accounts, ads, cables, or developer tools needed.

INSTALL

1. On your laptop, extract the ZIP and open NotebookScanner-0.5-Windows-Setup.exe.
   Install it, then open Notebook Scanner from your Start Menu.
   This early Windows build is unsigned, so Windows may show a publisher warning.
   Only install a copy you received from Sebastian. If security software or your
   organization blocks it, ask Sebastian; do not disable security protection.

2. On your Android phone, open NotebookScanner-0.5.apk to install it.
   Download/extract the ZIP on the phone too, or transfer just the APK to it.
   Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager.
   No developer mode is needed. Open Notebook Scanner after installation.

3. Turn Bluetooth on on both devices. In the normal Bluetooth settings, pair
   your phone and laptop and check that the pairing codes match.

4. On your phone, allow Nearby devices permission if asked. Choose your laptop
   in the scanner's Settings and tap Connect to this laptop.
   On Windows, choose your phone and click Connect. Approve access if asked.

TAKE A PHOTO

5. On the phone, tap Camera. Take a photo, then accept the camera's confirmation.
   Wait for the scanner to say it has been copied on your laptop.
   Click your unsent ChatGPT draft and press Ctrl+V. Send the message yourself.

After setup, open both apps in either order. They remember the devices; the phone
opens its usual camera automatically. Keep the laptop awake and phone unlocked.
Bluetooth must be on; Wi-Fi is not needed for the transfer. Downloading the apps
or using online ChatGPT can still require internet. You can test an offline paste
in Paint instead. The apps do not automatically open each other.

SETTINGS

On the phone, press Back from the camera to reach the scanner's Settings.
To get completion notifications, tap Allow completion notifications there.
On Windows, Settings has a notification checkbox and Set keyboard shortcut.
Notifications mean copied to your clipboard, not sent to ChatGPT.
An interrupted photo is not automatically resent: reconnect and retake it.

UPDATES AND REMOVAL

Install later versions over these apps; keep receiving updates from Sebastian.
If you already installed an earlier 0.4 development APK, it uses a different
signing certificate. You must uninstall that OLD phone app before installing 0.5,
then choose your laptop again. This clears the old app's saved preferences.
This is only the transition from the old development build, not normal updates.
Close older portable Windows copies before using the installed version.
Remove Notebook Scanner in Windows Settings > Apps, or uninstall normally on
Android. Windows saved preferences are retained for a future reinstall.
